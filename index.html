About Mac Urban

Built with purpose. Built to last.

Mac Urban Civil Construction delivers carefully considered residential and construction projects across Melbourne. Our approach combines construction knowledge, architectural thinking and disciplined project management to create homes that are beautiful, functional and built for long-term value.

What We Do

Construction services with a higher standard.

01

Custom Homes

We create individually designed homes that respond to your lifestyle, site conditions and architectural vision. From early planning through construction and completion, we focus on quality workmanship, practical detailing, material selection and long-term functionality.

02

New Homes

Our new home construction service provides a coordinated pathway from initial concept and documentation through approvals, construction and final handover. We manage trades, scheduling, quality control and project coordination to deliver a carefully built home with clarity throughout the construction process.

03

Renovations

We undertake considered renovations and extensions that improve both the character and functionality of existing homes. Our team carefully coordinates demolition, structural work, new finishes and services while respecting the original building and creating a seamless result between old and new spaces.

04

Commercial

We provide professional construction services for selected commercial, retail and development projects. Our approach focuses on programme management, site coordination, workmanship and communication, helping clients maintain control over quality, timelines and construction requirements throughout the project.

05

Project Management

Our project management service brings together consultants, trades, suppliers, budgets and construction schedules under one coordinated process. We monitor project progress, resolve construction issues, manage communication and maintain attention to quality so each stage can move forward efficiently and with clear accountability.

06

Construction Consulting

We provide practical construction advice for clients, developers and property owners at different stages of a project. From early feasibility and planning through procurement, budgeting and construction strategy, we help identify potential risks, improve decision-making and establish a clearer pathway towards successful project delivery.
