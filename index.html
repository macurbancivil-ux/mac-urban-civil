What We Do

Construction services.

From bespoke residential construction to commercial projects, Mac Urban provides practical construction solutions supported by professional coordination and project management.

01

Custom Homes

We deliver bespoke homes tailored to the site, architectural vision and lifestyle of each client. From early planning and consultant coordination through detailed construction and final handover, our team manages quality, workmanship and communication to create refined residences built with lasting performance, functionality and attention to detail.

02

New Homes

Our new home construction service provides a structured pathway from design development and approvals through procurement, construction and handover. We coordinate consultants, trades, materials, budgets and construction programs to maintain quality and accountability while delivering a well-managed building process aligned with the project's requirements.

03

Renovations

We undertake renovations and extensions with careful consideration of existing structures, architectural intent and day-to-day functionality. Our team manages site conditions, sequencing, trades, finishes and construction coordination to minimise disruption and deliver considered improvements that integrate seamlessly with the existing property while maintaining high standards of workmanship.

04

Commercial

Our commercial construction capability supports retail, office, development and other commercial projects with a strong focus on program, coordination, quality and site management. We work with clients, consultants, contractors and stakeholders to establish clear project requirements and deliver construction works in a controlled and professionally managed environment.

05

Project Management

We provide end-to-end project management covering consultant coordination, procurement, cost control, construction programming, contractor management, site communication and quality oversight. Our approach provides clients with clear visibility throughout the project, helping manage risks, resolve issues efficiently and maintain alignment between design, budget, program and construction delivery.

06

Construction Consulting

Our construction consulting service provides practical support across feasibility, estimating, planning, buildability, procurement and project strategy. Drawing on construction experience and industry knowledge, we help clients identify risks, evaluate options and make informed decisions before and during construction, improving project clarity and supporting more efficient delivery.

How We Work

Our process.

A structured approach helps create clarity from the first conversation through construction completion and handover.

01

Consultation

We begin by understanding the client's objectives, site conditions, budget, design expectations and project constraints. Early discussions help establish a practical project brief, identify key risks and clarify the scope required before design, approvals, consultant engagement and construction planning commence.

02

Planning

We coordinate architectural, engineering and specialist consultants while progressing approvals, documentation and construction planning. The objective is to establish a coordinated project pathway, identify interfaces early and ensure design decisions, statutory requirements, budgets and construction methodology are considered before works commence.

03

Pre-Construction

Before construction starts, we finalise contracts, specifications, procurement requirements, budgets, construction programs and site planning. Detailed preparation allows responsibilities, milestones and expectations to be clearly established, reducing uncertainty and creating a controlled transition from design and planning into physical construction.

04

Construction

During construction, our team manages site coordination, trades, quality control, program performance, materials and project communication. We monitor progress against agreed requirements, address construction issues promptly and maintain coordination between the client, consultants and contractors throughout the delivery phase.

05

Handover

At completion, we coordinate final inspections, defect management, documentation and handover requirements. The project is reviewed against the agreed scope and construction standards, with outstanding items addressed systematically to support a smooth transition from construction completion to client occupation or operational use.
