About Mac Urban

Construction built around quality.

Mac Urban Civil Construction is a Melbourne-based construction company focused on delivering high-quality residential and commercial projects.

We combine practical construction knowledge, contemporary design thinking and disciplined project management to create spaces that are built for everyday living and long-term value.

From new homes and luxury residences to renovations and commercial construction, our team works closely with clients, consultants and trades throughout every stage of the project.

Our approach is straightforward: communicate clearly, build professionally and maintain a high standard of quality from the first conversation through to final handover.

What We Do

Construction services
with a considered approach.

From individual renovations to complete residential and commercial projects, Mac Urban provides professional construction services with a strong focus on quality, communication and project control.

01

Custom Homes

Bespoke homes designed and constructed around the way you live. We coordinate planning, consultants, trades and construction to deliver a refined home with quality workmanship and attention to detail.

02

New Homes

Complete new-home construction from pre-construction planning through to handover. Our structured approach helps manage quality, timelines, costs and communication throughout the build.

03

Renovations

Thoughtfully managed renovations that improve function, appearance and value. We work carefully within existing structures while delivering contemporary finishes and practical construction solutions.

04

Commercial

Professional construction services for commercial environments, with careful coordination of consultants, trades, documentation, program requirements and construction standards.

05

Project Management

Clear project coordination from planning through completion. We manage construction activities, communication, scheduling and quality control to keep projects moving efficiently and professionally.

06

Construction Consulting

Practical construction advice for clients, developers and project teams. We provide experience-led guidance around buildability, construction planning, coordination and project delivery.
